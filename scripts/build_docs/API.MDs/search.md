search

Searching the Attack Surface Intelligence data lake is at the core of Attack Surface Intelligence. In milliseconds you can learn about everything from all exposed Internet addresses with port 22 open, a tie to the Lazarus Group threat actor, and within the financial services industry. Or you could find all passwords in known leaked databases that have a particular company's email tied to them. The opportunities are endless, and we encourage you to explore!

For a comprehensive guide to using the search endpoints in the Attack Surface Intelligence API, see our [Knowledge base article](https://support.securityscorecard.com/hc/en-us/articles/9616223335323).
