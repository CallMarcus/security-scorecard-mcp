All Companies \[New\]
=====================

/\*! tailwindcss v4.1.6 | MIT License | https://tailwindcss.com \*/ @layer theme, base, components, utilities; @layer utilities;

The All Companies API endpoints offer capabilities for managing monitored (partial & full) companies, including retrieving, updating, and deleting individual company details, alongside robust bulk operations for vendor details, tags, and notes with various filtering options.

**Note**: customers who do not have unlimited partial monitoring on their instance will need to add companies to a [portfolio](/reference/portfolios) prior to adding metadata about them.
